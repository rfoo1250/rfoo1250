Video Games

From real-time strategy to open-world RPGs, games have been a constant source of engagement and inspiration throughout my life.

Overview

Games have shaped how I think about systems, feedback loops, and decision-making under constraints. I'm drawn to titles that reward strategic thinking and long-term planning — whether that's managing resources across a campaign or optimizing a team composition. The design principles behind well-crafted games map surprisingly well onto software engineering.

Beyond the strategy genre, I also enjoy story-driven RPGs and games with rich worldbuilding. Arknights in particular has been a long-running interest — its blend of tower defense mechanics, operator synergies, and expansive lore keeps it engaging across many hours of play.
