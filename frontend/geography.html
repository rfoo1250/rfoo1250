Geography

Maps, borders, and the stories behind them. Understanding how geography shapes politics, culture, and history gives me broader context for the world.

Overview

Geography has always fascinated me — not just in the sense of knowing where places are, but understanding why borders exist where they do, how terrain dictates the rise and fall of civilizations, and how physical geography drives political and economic outcomes. Growing up in Malaysia gave me an early appreciation for how diverse a relatively small region of the world can be.

I spend time studying maps, reading about geopolitics, and playing geography quiz games. The interconnectedness of physical and human geography — how rivers become borders, how mountain ranges define trade routes, how coastlines shape empires — is endlessly interesting to me and informs how I think about systems more broadly.
