Military Strategy

Reading about historical campaigns and strategic doctrine feeds my interest in decision-making under pressure, resource management, and complex systems.

Overview

Military history and strategic doctrine have long captivated me — not for the violence, but for the complexity. Great campaigns are exercises in logistics, information asymmetry, and decision-making under uncertainty. Understanding how commanders throughout history solved these problems reveals universal principles that translate well beyond the battlefield.

I'm particularly interested in WWII grand strategy, the operational level of war, and how logistics shapes the outcome of conflicts more than tactics do. Games like Hearts of Iron IV scratch this itch by letting me model and experiment with strategic decisions at scale — it's a natural extension of the same systems thinking I apply to software problems.
